Doom engine (Chocolate Doom, WebAssembly)

Plays the free Freedoom game data (BSD-3-Clause). Click the game once it starts, then use the arrow keys, Ctrl to fire, Space to open, Shift to run.

Freedoom: Phase 1 – single player episodes (Doom 1 / Ultimate Doom style)
Freedoom: Phase 2 – 32 levels (Doom 2 style)

Engine: cloudflare/doom-wasm, GPL-2.0, from Chocolate Doom. Multiplayer is not available here.
